What questions to ask before you commit to a platform?

Before committing to a voice platform, technical leaders must question vendor architecture regarding model deployment location, real-time pricing transparency, and native handling of regional languages. Decision-makers should demand explicit operational metrics regarding latency under load, data retention policies, and exact compute metering mechanics.
